One Platform. Every Role. Total Clarity.

Genesis is a cloud native, intelligent enterprise investment management platform that unifies data, workflows, and teams across your entire investment office. By eliminating fragmented systems and real time data gaps, Genesis gives portfolio managers, traders, and operations teams a single source of truth—powering faster decisions, cleaner execution, and scalable growth.

Genesis is a unified, cloud-native investment management solution that connects your entire investment office on a single, trusted data platform. Built for the way firms serving private and institutional investors — or both - work today, Genesis eliminates silos and enables real-time interoperability across the entire investment lifecycle including, portfolio management, trading, accounting, and reporting.

Unified Applications for the Modern Investment Office

Genesis delivers seamlessly connected applications—each purpose-built to empower every role across your investment office. Data Analytics & Insights: Advanced analytics and visualization tools for portfolio analysis and decision-making. Portfolio Management: Model, construct, and rebalance portfolios at scale, with real-time scenario analysis, exception monitoring, and support for multi-asset, multi-sleeve, and multi-currency strategies.

Trading & Compliance: OMS workflows with real-time compliance checks, market connectivity, and seamless market data integration. Accounting & Operations: Complete IBOR/ABOR functionality for accurate, real-time valuations and reconciliations. Managed operations offerings free up time.
